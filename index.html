About the Author

Akintunde Olumide

Akintunde Olumide is a preacher, teacher, author, song writer and producer. His Christian journey began during his undergraduate days at the Federal University of Technology, Akure, where he served as the Public Relations Officer of the Joint Campus Christian Fellowship in 2003.

In 2005, he was privileged to serve as the Central Prayer Secretary of the Nigeria Christian Corpers' Fellowship, Enugu State. Over the years, he has held several leadership roles in notable Christian organizations before pioneering Victorious Vine International in Akure, Ondo State.

In 2018, he also served as Media Director with the Pentecostal Fellowship of Nigeria (PFN), Ondo State. Today, he reaches a wider audience through his weekly broadcast, FAITHBOOST, on various social media platforms.

Beyond ministry, Olumide is involved in the creative technology industry. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Engineering from the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), and a Postgraduate degree in Electronics and Computer Engineering from Lagos State University (LASU). Also, he attended Word of Faith Bible Institute. He is happily married and blessed with children.
